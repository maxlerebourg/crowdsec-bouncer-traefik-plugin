--type restricts to a decision type (ie. ban,captcha).
--scope restricts to this scope (ie. ip,range,session).
--value example: 1.2.3.4,userName.
--ip / --range are shorthands for --scope ip|range --value.
session appears only as a help example, not as a dedicated flag.
